# Site notes: The Garage King

- **Client / domain:** The Garage King, thegarageking.com (current site on Divi; replaced at launch).
- **Started from template:** waws-wp-site-template at 6950b64 (2026-10-17).
- **Prototype link:** works only while this repo is public. Repo is private by default. Generate the blueprint with `python3 scripts/make-blueprint.py` after changing theme files; the link is
  `https://playground.wordpress.net/?blueprint-url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/we-are-web-services/site-thegarageking/main/blueprint.json`
- **Raw client material:** Drive, `Client Work/Second Life Enterprises LLC/The Garage King/Website Build/New Website Build Materials` (reorganized 2026-10-23; the client is under the agency Second Life Enterprises). The brief is `docs/brief.md`.
- **Decisions and why:** block theme and core blocks, not Divi (design in files, no licence in a public repo). Source order: questionnaire and current site decide content; the mockup is design reference only.
- **Plugins added beyond the standard list:** none yet. Gravity Forms (client's current form tool) is paid and is not in the prototype.
- **Real content pass (2026-10-17):** text and photos from the current site were copied into the prototype so the client can see a fuller site (David's request). Online sales (shop, cart, checkout, account, products) were left out on purpose. Pages: Home, Overhead Racks, Slatwall, Steel Cabinets (kept at `/steel-cabinets`, the existing URL; the site says "coming soon"), Pricing, Photo Gallery (34 photos), Testimonials, Contact, Privacy Policy, `/ga`, `/nextdoor` and `/sael`, plus the 21 blog posts. Pages that do not exist on the current site (How It Works, FAQ, About, Service Areas, Solutions hub, Motorized, Floor Coatings, Ventilation, Book a consultation) were built from the Website Content Outline & Questionnaire and are flagged on the page. Business phones, email, hours and showroom address come from the current site's public Contact page. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/import_content.py`, `python3 scripts/build_pages.py`, `python3 scripts/make-blueprint.py --php`.
- **Pricing (2026-10-23):** the Pricing page shows regular prices, add-ons, product details and three package cards (names only). The price estimator built on 2026-10-17 was **removed from scope on 2026-10-23** (David: nothing on the current site to match it; the client has not confirmed having the information to base estimates on). Its last version is in git history (commit e1a9cfd).
- **Forms (2026-10-23):** the plugin `gk-leads` (not the theme, so changing the theme never loses it) provides `[gk_quote_form source="quote|ads|contact"]` and `[gk_scheduler]`, a private "Leads" screen in wp-admin and spam traps (hidden field and minimum fill time, no visible puzzle). Where leads go: stored in WordPress only, unless `GK_LEAD_WEBHOOK` (POSTs each lead as JSON, for GoHighLevel, Zapier or similar) and/or `GK_LEAD_EMAIL` are defined in `wp-config.php`. Neither is set. No photo upload, no estimate, no reCAPTCHA (David, 2026-10-23). Forms end where the live site's end (`docs/tracking-inventory.md`): the Quote and Ads forms on `/ga-thank-you-page-garage-talk/`, the Contact form on its own page.
- **First full draft (2026-10-23):** David's direction: forms are the main call to action (phone number only in contact areas); positioning line "boutique, family owned and operated expert in luxury full garage renovations, including floors, doors and storage solution systems"; "Overhead Storage Solutions", "Garage Ventilation", keep "Motorized Overhead Racks" and "Slatwall" as headings; add Lifts, Garage Door Installation and Repairs and a Project Stories "Coming soon" page; one service area statement ("We serve all Greater Austin, Greater San Antonio and surrounding areas") with a "Don't see your town?" call to action. Pricing now lives on the Pricing page (regular prices from the client's Latest Deals document, read 2026-10-23; limited-time offers are not published). Quote form in the live site's two steps (form, then the scheduler on the thank-you page); Contact form matched to the live Gravity Form (plugin 0.4.0). Estimator and photo upload dropped. Details in `docs/next-session.md`.
- **Not seen or not done:** the blueprint is untested in real Playground (the test tool needs Node 24; the build was checked in a local WordPress 7.1 with SQLite and Chromium screenshots). Before/after slider, price estimator, forms, scheduler, pop-up, GoHighLevel and Jobber links, real photos and video, and pages other than Home and Overhead Storage are not built.
